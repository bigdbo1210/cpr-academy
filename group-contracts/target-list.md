# CPR Group Contracts — Target List

**Business:** Don't Be Ordinary CPR, AED & First Aid Training, LLC — Louisville, KY
**Built:** October 26, 2026 · **Status:** Research complete. Nothing on this list has been contacted. All outreach drafts only, pending Derrick's approval.

## How this list was built

- 36 real Louisville-area employers, each verified against a public listing or the business's own website on 2026-10-26 (name, address/area, phone).
- Every business on the list employs people who must hold current CPR / BLS / First Aid certification to do their jobs or to keep the employer in compliance:
  - **Childcare & Head Start** — Kentucky childcare licensing requires certified CPR/first aid coverage on staff; Head Start follows the same standard.
  - **Home health & home care** — aides and nurses work alone in homes; agencies require CPR/BLS for employment and accreditation.
  - **Assisted living / memory care / adult day health** — direct-care staff must be able to respond; certification is a hiring and survey-readiness standard.
  - **Dental** — BLS is the standing standard for dental teams, and required where sedation is offered.
  - **Physical therapy** — clinic clinical staff hold CPR/BLS as a condition of practice.
  - **Autism therapy centers** — RBTs and clinical staff work with medically vulnerable children; CPR/first aid is a standard credential.
  - **Warehouse / manufacturing** — OSHA expects trained first-aid responders where there is no nearby clinic/hospital response; large sites run responder/EHS teams.
- **Existing-client note:** Erin Priddy (head nurse at a daycare for children with autism) is already a client. Her employer's name isn't recorded, so no organization was excluded by name; the closest matching organizations — Hopebridge (2 centers), Skillz 4 Life, and Home of the Innocents — are included because they run the same staff-certification model.
- **Public emails are scarce by design of these businesses:** only 3 of 36 publish a direct email. Everyone else is phone or website-contact-form only. No email on this list was guessed or constructed — if it isn't shown, it wasn't publicly listed.

## Working notes

- **Who to ask for:** director, office manager, administrator, or whoever owns staff training/certifications. Front desks route this well if you say "staff CPR certification."
- **Best calling windows:** childcare — mid-morning after drop-off rush or early afternoon nap time for a callback request; agencies and clinics — weekday business hours; senior living — ask for the administrator or staff-development nurse.
- **Quoting (INTERNAL ONLY — Derrick's rule, set 2026-10-27):** outreach copy states NO prices, because Derrick quotes each group by headcount and offers group discounts at his discretion depending on the number of people. Internal anchor when he builds a written quote: standard in-person BLS is $130/person ($150 online). Every quote is issued per group, in writing, after the headcount is known. (His long-standing Friday employer is grandfathered at ~$100/person — a legacy rate he has chosen not to raise; never quote it to new prospects.)
