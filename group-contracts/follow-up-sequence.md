**Rule:** two written follow-ups is the limit. No pressure language, no guilt, no fake deadlines. Every message leaves the door open.

## Phone-call opener script (for phone/web-only businesses)

*Target length: under a minute. Goal is not to sell the class on the first call — it's to reach the person who owns staff certifications and earn permission to send the one-page pitch.*

**Opening (front desk / receptionist):**

> "Hi, my name is Derrick Dennis with Don't Be Ordinary CPR here in Louisville. I'm an American Heart Association instructor, and I do on-site CPR and BLS certification for staff teams — childcare, home health, dental, senior living. I'm trying to reach whoever handles your staff's CPR certifications — would that be your director, or your office manager?"

**If transferred / when the right person is on:**

> "Thanks for taking the call. Quick reason I'm calling: your [teachers / care team / clinical staff] have to keep current CPR and BLS cards, and I run group sessions right at your location — five to seven people at a time, priced by the size of the group, and everybody leaves with their AHA eCard. I teach evenings and Saturdays, so it doesn't pull your team off the floor during the day. I'm not asking you to book anything today — can I send you a one-page sheet with the training details and my open dates, and work up a quote for your group's size? Then you can look it over with your team."

**If they give an email:** confirm spelling letter by letter, send the short version of the pitch the same day, and note the date next to their row in `target-list.csv`.

**If they say "we handle that in-house / already have someone":**

> "Understood — a lot of places do. If it ever helps to have a backup for new-hire classes or a month when your calendar is full, I'm local and I can usually get a group in within a couple of weeks. Mind if I leave my number? It's (502) 835-8535, Derrick Dennis, Don't Be Ordinary CPR."

**If they're mid-renewal-crunch / interested now:**

> "I can hold a date for you this week. My group sessions run Monday through Thursday 4 to 9, Friday morning or evening, and Saturday 9 to 3. Which of those fits your team best? Once we pick it, I'll put the quote in writing for your group of [number] — and your date is locked."

**Voicemail version (leave once, then switch to the Day 4 / Day 10 written cadence if an email exists):**

> "Hi, this is Derrick Dennis with Don't Be Ordinary CPR in Louisville, (502) 835-8535. I teach on-site AHA CPR and BLS group classes for staff teams — group pricing by headcount, eCards included, evenings and Saturdays available. If your team's certifications are coming due, I'd be glad to work you up a quote. Again, (502) 835-8535. Thank you."

## Tracking

After each touch, record the date and outcome (sent / called / contact name / callback date) in the Notes column of `target-list.csv`. A "not now" with a renewal month noted is a future booking — park it with the month in the Notes column and re-approach 60 days before those cards expire.
