## Exam question (Derrick asked Nov 15) — answered from AHA HeartCode FAQ (cpr.heart.org)
- The exam is INSIDE the online course: students work the patient cases, then take the multiple-choice exam within the online program (84% minimum pass). The ACLS precourse self-assessment is also built into HeartCode ACLS. No separate exam purchase — the $178 license covers the online portion including its exam.
- AHA FAQ: "Successful completion of both a HeartCode Online course and a hands-on skills session is required to receive an American Heart Association course completion eCard, valid for two years." The hands-on session is sold separately (instructor-led or simulation station). Online portion alone -> certificate of completion, which the student presents for entry into a skills session; instructors verify certificates at eLearning.heart.org/verify_certificate.
