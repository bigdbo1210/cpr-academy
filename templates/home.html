Questions People Ask

How much is a BLS class? BLS is $150. Heartsaver CPR AED is $155, First Aid is $95, and a skills check-off is $80. Every course shows its full price in the catalog below — no surprises.

How does blended learning work? Complete the online coursework and assessment first, then book a short in-person skills session with your instructor (20–30 minutes). Pass both, and you are certified.

How do I get my card? After successful completion, AHA courses issue an AHA eCard; our independently developed courses issue a Don't Be Ordinary CPR certificate. Every course page states plainly which document you receive before you enroll.

Do you train groups at our location? Yes — on-site group training for childcare centers, schools, churches, and businesses across Louisville and Jefferson County. Call or text (502) 835-8535 for a group quote.
