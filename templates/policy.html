Sales & Rescheduling Policy

Don't Be Ordinary CPR, AED & First Aid Training, LLC · Louisville, Kentucky · Policy version {{ policy_version }}

All Sales Are Final — No Voluntary Refunds

Once payment is successfully processed, course purchases, digital access, training registrations, and skills-session reservations are nonrefundable, except where required by applicable law or a billing error on our part.

No refunds are given for:

  • Changing your mind
  • Finding a cheaper course elsewhere
  • Selecting the wrong course
  • Failing to attend
  • Not having time to complete coursework
  • Deciding not to take the course
  • Failing an assessment or practical evaluation
  • Missing an in-person skills appointment
  • Failing to complete the course

We do not offer cancellation windows, satisfaction guarantees, or price-matching refunds, and there is no automatic refund or transfer if you select the wrong course — please read the course disclosures on each course page before enrolling, and call us at (502) 835-8535 if you are unsure which course you need.

Required Exceptions — We Will Make It Right

The following are reviewed and resolved by the business, and any refund legally owed is paid in full (we never deduct processing fees from a refund you are legally entitled to):

  • Duplicate charges
  • Incorrect charges caused by us
  • Failure on our part to deliver purchased training or restore required access
  • Unauthorized transactions, disputes, or refunds required by law or payment-network rules

To request a review, call (502) 835-8535. Every administrative refund action is documented.

Rescheduling a Skills Session

  • Each paid skills-session reservation includes one complimentary reschedule, requested at least 24 hours before the scheduled appointment, through your booking on this site.
  • Requests with less than 24 hours' notice do not qualify for the complimentary reschedule.
  • No-shows and late cancellations forfeit the reserved session; a new appointment after forfeiture requires a new booking and payment.
  • If we cancel an appointment, you receive a complimentary replacement session; if we cannot deliver the purchased service, we process the refund legally owed.
  • All appointment times are Louisville time (America/New_York).

Before You Buy

Each course page states the course format (online-only, blended, or skills-only), its prerequisites, any required in-person session, and the exact document you will receive on completion. Our completion certificates are issued by Don't Be Ordinary CPR, AED & First Aid Training, LLC and are not AHA eCards. Verify with your employer or licensing body that a course meets your requirement before purchasing.

Questions about billing or this policy: (502) 835-8535 · 240 Whittington Parkway, Louisville, KY 40222.
