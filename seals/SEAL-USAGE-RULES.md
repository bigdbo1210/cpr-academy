## Derrick's seal: "Aligned Instructor" (for an instructor aligned with a Training Center)
Files: seals/Instructor Seals/ (RGB PNG for web; CMYK JPG/EPS for print). Use AS-IS — the design cannot be altered in any way; show it in its entirety; never screened back or as background art.

## Non-negotiables for his use
1. REQUIRED AFFILIATION LINE: the Instructor seal must always appear with the line "Aligned with [TC name] in [City, State, Country]" and the TC name/logo relevant to that material must be shown. (OPEN: the exact TC name/city is not in Debo's records — Derrick to supply.)
2. NEVER ALONE: must sit with his business name/logo; seal must be SMALLER than that name/logo (ideally 50% or less) and separated by white space of at least 1 inch or the seal's width, whichever is larger. Clear space around the seal at all times = the width of the heart in the AHA logo. Minimum size: heart/torch must exceed 3/8 inch.
3. ONLY for AHA ECC/CPR/First Aid courses — marketing/promotion of AHA training, using AHA materials, per current Guidelines. NEVER on independent (non-AHA) training or materials he created himself (so: NOT beside Shaken Baby, Babysitter, or other house courses). Materials must read as issued by the instructor/business, not by the AHA.
4. ALLOWED placements: AHA course schedules and announcements; brochures/flyers/posters about AHA courses; temporary auto signage; AHA course advertisements (print and online); a web page that lists or advertises AHA ECC courses; directory ads; email signatures (seal secondary to TC name/logo; optional secondary line "Proud to be an AHA Instructor").
5. PROHIBITED placements ("under no circumstances"): letterhead; BUSINESS CARDS; permanent building signage; specialty items (cups, pens, shirts); non-AHA supplementary materials, textbooks, charts, course outlines. (The printed list also carries an "any advertisement or notice" item that tensions against the permitted AHA-course ads and worked examples — confine ad use to AHA ECC course advertising per the examples; confirm with the TC if in doubt.)
6. Basis: US-based TC/TCC/TS/Instructors only; TCC provides the correct seal to its sites/instructors and revokes use when someone leaves. Use only while the TC Agreement/alignment is in effect; on termination, remove everywhere. AHA audits use with random samples; the TC carries primary enforcement; violations can end the TC Agreement.
