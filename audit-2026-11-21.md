# CPR Academy vs. AHA Audit — Nov 21, 2026
Read-only audit of cpr-academy.onrender.com (13 courses + policy/disclosure pages) against elearning.heart.org and shopcpr.heart.org, at Derrick's direction ("fine tooth comb — make sure what we offer lines up with the American Heart, the wording and everything").

## CRITICAL
1. **ACLS ($318) and PALS ($318) — OVERCLAIM.** Academy sells both as "100% online, no in-person class or skills session" with "eCard available immediately after completion." AHA's HeartCode ACLS/PALS ($178 each) state "Requires Hands-On Session" — the eCard issues only after the online portion AND a hands-on session. The Academy's own AHA Disclosure page does NOT list ACLS/PALS among eCard courses, so the course pages contradict the site's own disclosure. ACLS page also softens AHA prerequisites ("No clinical experience required — you only need a current BLS card" vs AHA's BLS proficiency / rhythm recognition / ECG / pharmacology expectations).
2. **Heartsaver self-contradiction (CPR AED $160, Pediatric $160, First Aid $100).** Each page's fact box says the document is "a certificate issued by Don't Be Ordinary CPR Training, not an AHA eCard," while the paragraph below says it IS "an official American Heart Association Heartsaver course" with an official AHA eCard. The Disclosure page sides with the eCard version — the fact boxes are the wrong text.

## IMPORTANT
3. **No combined Heartsaver First Aid CPR AED course on the Academy.** AHA sells it as Heartsaver Total ($24.50 online portion). The Academy splits it ($160 CPR AED + $100 First Aid) — which also conflicts with Derrick's settled rule (Nov 21) that the combined class is $160 flat.
4. **"BLS for Healthcare Providers" is AHA's retired pre-2020 name.** Current name: "BLS Provider" / "HeartCode BLS." Applies to the $150 blended and $150 instructor-led listings. Format/eCard claims otherwise match AHA.
5. **"OSHA Bloodborne Pathogens" ($90)** — AHA's course is "Heartsaver Bloodborne Pathogens" ($23); "OSHA" is not in the name (AHA: "designed to meet OSHA requirements… when paired with site-specific instruction"). Academy description copies AHA's text but lists the course as its own non-AHA course in the Disclosure. "Certificate valid for 1 year" is an added claim not on AHA's page. No-skills-test matches AHA.
6. **"CPR Instructor Course" ($600)** overstates scope — the content is AHA's BLS Instructor Essentials path (online Essentials $43.90 + classroom + monitoring + printed BLS Instructor Manual included). It is a BLS instructor course, not a general CPR instructor credential. Disclosure ("not an AHA eCard") is accurate.
7. **First Aid page** uses OSHA-requirements wording AHA attaches to Heartsaver Total, not to First Aid alone.
8. **Heartsaver pages add a "25-question exam… 80% or higher"** that AHA's course pages do not state.
9. **Footer claim to verify:** homepage footer says "American Heart Association-authorized Training Center" — confirm this matches Derrick's actual AHA status/alignment before it stays.
